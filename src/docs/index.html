About LivingSpeech

What it is

LivingSpeech helps people who can't speak, or can't always speak to communicate. Use it to order a coffee, answer a question, or join a conversation.

How it works

  1. Open LivingSpeech.fyi
  2. Type what you want to say.
  3. Press Return, or tap the play button. Your device speaks it.

Phrases you've said recently stay on screen so you can say them again with one tap. Four quick buttons, which you can change, speak common answers like "Yes" and "No" right away.

Put it on your home screen

LivingSpeech is a web page that works like an app. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen with one tap, works without an internet connection, and keeps itself up to date when it has one. There's nothing to buy and no app store account needed.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open LivingSpeech.fyi in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button . In Safari it's at the bottom of the screen, or at the top on iPad. You may need to tap ⋯ first.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Open LivingSpeech.fyi in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button ⋮ in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.
  4. Open the app, go to Settings, and choose a voice. On Android the default voice often doesn't work until you pick one.

It's free

LivingSpeech costs nothing. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no "premium" version. I wrote it, because I needed it. It's free and open source software, so anyone can see how it works, or make their own copy.

It's private

Everything you type stays on your device. It is never sent to us, and we can never see it.

There's no account to sign up for, no tracking. The whole app runs on your own device, and the voices come from your device too. The only copy of what you've said is the recent list on your screen, which you can clear whenever you like.
